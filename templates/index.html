Welcome to the UNIZULU Student Complaints and Grievance Portal

This portal is designed to streamline the process of submitting and tracking student complaints and grievances. Whether you have an academic concern, housing issue, or any other matter, our system ensures your voice is heard.

About the System

The Student Complaints and Grievance Portal is designed to help students raise issues quickly, categorise them correctly, and monitor how each matter is being handled from submission to resolution.

01

Complaint Submission

Students can submit complaints related to academics, ICT, housing, or finance. Every complaint receives a unique reference number for easy tracking.

02

Smart Routing

The system helps students select the right complaint category and directs cases to the correct department or support channel.

03

Monitoring & Reporting

Staff and administrators can review trends, view complaint status, and generate analytics to improve service delivery and response times.
